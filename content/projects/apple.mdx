## iPhone X launch pages

I worked with Apple’s marketing and design teams to build responsive landing pages for the iPhone X launch in November 2017. I translated the team’s static design specifications into responsive front-end code under the launch deadlines.
## CSS-animated email campaign

I prototyped and developed a CSS-animated direct email campaign. CSS supplied the motion without JavaScript; implementation had to account for differences between email clients.
## Campaign tooling

Campaign production involved repeated manual steps. I built command-line tools in Node.js, Bash, and Gulp to automate builds, previews, and deployment, giving the team a repeatable way to prepare an email campaign.
