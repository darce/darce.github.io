### iPhone X launch pages

For the November 2017 iPhone X launch, I worked with Apple’s marketing and design teams to turn static design specifications into responsive landing pages. The designs established the visual treatment. My responsibility was to make that treatment work in the browser under the launch deadlines.

I had to preserve the intended composition as the available width changed. The supplied layouts established the visual target; responsive code had to account for the sizes between them as well. My part of the launch was that implementation, working from the design team’s specifications.

### CSS-animated email campaign

I also prototyped and developed a CSS-animated direct email campaign. The email itself carried the motion, without JavaScript. That introduced a different implementation constraint from the web pages: email clients varied in the CSS they supported.

I used the prototype to work through the motion before developing the campaign implementation. Email-client compatibility was part of that work: an animation that worked on a web page still had to be considered within the more restricted environment of an email.

### Campaign tooling

Preparing campaigns involved repeated build, preview, and deployment steps. I wrote command-line tools in Node.js, Bash, and Gulp to automate that preparation and give the team a repeatable production process.

The automation replaced repeated manual preparation with defined commands. It gave the team a way to prepare another campaign through the same build and preview process, with deployment steps included in the tooling.
