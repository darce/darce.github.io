## A visual treatment drawn from the films

Filmmaker Debra McCall wanted a new website for the films of her 1982 reconstruction of Oskar Schlemmer’s Bauhaus Dances. She supplied the films; I designed and implemented their web presentation.

The visual direction grew out of the film’s Hoop Dance sequence, where white rings create stark geometric forms against a dark stage. I used that imagery as the basis for the site’s visual system and interaction language.

![Reinfentanz performance. A dancer stands on one foot with her left foot stretched back, holding five white rings. Two rings form concentric circles near her head, three form concentric circles below her hips.](/images/bauhaus-dances-hoop-dance.jpg)

A subtle ring-based interaction, derived from the Hoop Dance imagery, adds motion without overpowering the content.

## Implementation

I built a custom WordPress theme on top of [Underscores](https://underscores.me/) so the client could manage content independently. I implemented self-hosted video and caching, so the film presentation did not depend on third-party embeds.
