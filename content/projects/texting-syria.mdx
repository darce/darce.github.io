Texting Syria brings photographs together with messages received by the people pictured. Photographer Liam Maloney supplied the images, text, and translations; I developed the experience end to end.

The exhibition invited visitors to connect to an SMS server and receive a series of actual messages on their own phones. Those messages had been received when Maloney took the photographs. The interaction extended the exhibition onto a device the visitor was already carrying.

The [Photoville NY 2016 record](https://photoville.com/projects/texting-syria/) credits Liam Maloney “with coding by Daniel Arce” and documents the SMS interaction. [Moving Walls 23](https://www.movingwalls.org/moving-walls/23/texting-syria.html) provides the photographs and Maloney’s statement about people in Lebanon keeping in touch with family in Syria.
