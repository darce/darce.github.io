## Bringing the conversations into the exhibition

Liam photographed Syrians who had fled to Lebanon and were keeping in touch with family in Homs by phone. The installation brought those photographs together with messages received at the time they were taken.

I wrote the application in PHP and integrated the Twilio API. Visitors connected to the exhibition’s SMS service and received a sequence of texts drawn from those earlier exchanges. Their own text-message inbox was the interface.

## The visitor’s phone as part of the installation

Using SMS made the visitor’s own phone part of the exhibition. The photographs showed people keeping in touch with family elsewhere; the messages brought the words of those exchanges onto a device the visitor used for their own conversations.

Texting Syria was exhibited at [Photoville New York in 2016](https://photoville.com/projects/texting-syria/). Liam’s photographs and artist statement are also documented in [Moving Walls 23](https://www.movingwalls.org/moving-walls/23/texting-syria.html).
