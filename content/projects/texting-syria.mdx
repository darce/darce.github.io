Texting Syria brings Liam Maloney’s photographs together with actual text messages. Maloney supplied the photographs, text, and translations; I developed the SMS experience end to end in 2014 using the Twilio API.

The exhibition invited visitors to connect to an SMS server and receive a series of actual messages on their own phones. According to Maloney’s project statement, those texts had been received when the photographs were taken.

Photoville NY exhibited the project in 2016. Its [exhibition record](https://photoville.com/projects/texting-syria/) credits Liam Maloney “with coding by Daniel Arce” and documents the SMS interaction. [Moving Walls 23](https://www.movingwalls.org/moving-walls/23/texting-syria.html) provides the photographs and Maloney’s statement about people in Lebanon keeping in touch with family in Syria.
