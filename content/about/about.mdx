<Intro>

<h2>Hello, I'm Daniel Arcé.</h2>

I'm a product designer and design technologist. I work from early product questions through interaction design, functional prototypes, and production, with accessibility built into those decisions.

</Intro>

### Making an idea tangible

My background is in interactive media and front-end development. That experience lets me turn a proposed interaction into something a team can try, question, and refine. A working prototype helps reveal what the design asks of the software and what the software asks of the person using it.

My recent independent work explores how AI can help people use visual collections. [Visual Search Workbench](/projects/visual-search-workbench/) lets someone start with a picture and follow similar images toward a creative direction. [AltContext](/projects/altcontext/) combines facial recognition with image description to give blind readers more context about who is pictured. Both projects bring questions about usefulness, uncertainty, and editorial control into the interface.

### Bringing the work to launch

At [PhotoShelter](/projects/photoshelter/), I combined product R&D with accessibility work on a large digital-asset-management platform. A workflow prototype exposed integration problems before production development, while my WCAG audit and remediation work helped address the requirements of enterprise customers. The case study gives the figures and the evidence behind them.

At [MSNBC](/projects/msnbc/), I helped take a dedicated video experience from initial product definition through working prototypes and interaction design to launch in six months. The work involved an internal team, external vendors, and stakeholders, with the aim of letting viewers browse coverage without interrupting playback.

### How I make design decisions

I use prototypes to make behaviour available for review, and accessibility checks to examine whether people can understand and operate it. I also maintain [Heuristics Canon](/research/heuristics-canon/), a collection of principles linked to published sources. It helps me explain why a design decision fits the problem and where that reasoning still needs evidence from use.

I'm interested in senior and lead product-design roles where I can help define a new experience, work closely with the people building it, and carry the design through delivery.
