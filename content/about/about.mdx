### Working with prototypes

I build prototypes so a team can try a proposed flow and compare alternatives. Using real content lets us check how the layout handles long titles or images in different proportions. I also use these prototypes to explore details such as loading and keyboard navigation with the people who will build and maintain the product.

### Independent products

[Visual Search Workbench](/projects/visual-search-workbench/) is a macOS app I'm developing for exploring photo collections. You can search with words or start with a picture you like, then follow similar images to find a direction for a creative project. It searches folders on your Mac and connected drives, with processing kept local.

[AltContext](/projects/altcontext/) combines facial recognition with image description to give blind readers more context about who is pictured. In a photograph of a band performing, for example, a description could name the singer as well as describe the scene. It uses identities confirmed by the publisher, with descriptions reviewed in WordPress. Processing runs on Oracle Cloud Infrastructure, with controls to export or delete the site's hosted recognition data.

### Bringing the work to launch

At [PhotoShelter](/projects/photoshelter/), I combined product R&D with accessibility work on a large platform for managing digital media. A workflow prototype exposed integration problems before production development. I also audited the interface against WCAG accessibility guidelines and worked on fixes to meet enterprise customers' requirements.

At [MSNBC](/projects/msnbc/), I helped take a dedicated video experience from product definition to launch in six months, working with the internal team and external vendors. I proposed collapsible panes so viewers could read comments and browse coverage while the video kept playing, then carried those interactions through working prototypes into production.

### How I approach decisions

During planning, I work through accessibility requirements and the behaviour a feature needs to support. I record those decisions and use automated checks to catch regressions as the implementation changes.

I maintain [Heuristics Canon](/research/heuristics-canon/), a collection of principles drawn from published work on design and software. I use it when reviewing a proposed solution and looking for alternatives. [WorkBay](/projects/workbay/) keeps project decisions and review findings available across sessions with AI coding tools, so I can continue the work without reconstructing its history.
