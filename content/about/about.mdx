<h2>Hello, I'm Daniel Arcé.</h2>

I'm a product designer and design technologist. My recurring specialty is turning technical complexity into understandable user-facing systems.

I've shipped user-facing software for more than 14 years across media, consumer platforms, enterprise SaaS, and independent AI products. That work has included MSNBC, Apple, PhotoShelter, and my own products.

Accessibility sharpened my discipline around real constraints and falsifiable product quality. Designs are checked against what a user can actually do.

Recent AI work extends that discipline to uncertainty, provenance, privacy, correction, and failure states. The product must show what it does not know.

My code fluency with React, TypeScript, SwiftUI, and Python lets product hypotheses meet real system constraints early. Working software is part of the design process, not a later phase.

What I bring:
- **Semantic Image Search** — a native macOS local-first AI photo search product designed and built end to end, with explicit incomplete-data states.
- **PhotoShelter** — R&D prototyping that surfaced integration issues early (an internal estimate of roughly 30% less downstream implementation effort) and WCAG remediation that protected $9.2M ARR across a platform with 80,000+ users.
- **MSNBC** — senior design technologist who prototyped and shipped the live video platform for msnbc.com.
- [**Heuristics Canon**](https://github.com/darce/heuristics-canon) — a versioned library of design and engineering rules with stable IDs, so a critique names the exact rule it leans on. It decided the visible media-pending state in Semantic Image Search.

### Independent product R&D

My independent products include AltContext (context-aware alt text for media systems), Semantic Image Search, and WorkBay.

I'm interested in product design for AI and complex systems, design technology, accessibility, media and content platforms.
