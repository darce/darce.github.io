### Working with prototypes

I build prototypes so a team can try a proposed flow and compare alternatives. Often, seeing a low-fidelity prototype can inform engineering solutions that prevent expensive mistakes down the road. Higher-fidelity prototypes present interactions that can be validated, and developers get a better feel of what designers intend to build in a way that static documentation cannot.

### Independent products

[Visual Search Workbench](/projects/visual-search-workbench/) is a macOS app I'm developing for exploring photo collections. You can search with words, or start with a picture you like, then follow similar images to find a direction for a creative project. It searches folders on your Mac, with all processing kept local; nothing gets uploaded anywhere.

[AltContext](/projects/altcontext/) combines facial recognition with image description to generate captions with context about who is pictured. In a photograph of a band performing, for example, a description could name the singer as well as describe the scene. It uses identities confirmed by the publisher, with descriptions reviewed in a WordPress admin page. Processing runs on Oracle Cloud Infrastructure (it's not yet practical to describe images locally), with controls to delete the site's hosted recognition data. Publishers maintain control of their data.

### Bringing the work to launch

At [PhotoShelter](/projects/photoshelter/), I combined product R&D with accessibility work on a large platform for managing digital media. A workflow prototype exposed integration problems before production development. I audited the interface against WCAG accessibility guidelines and worked on fixes to meet enterprise customers' requirements.

At [MSNBC](/projects/msnbc/), I helped take a dedicated video experience from product definition to launch in six months, working with the internal team and external vendors. Though video content interfaces that combine a social interactivity axis are common today, there were few examples to guied us back then. One soluition I prototyped were collapsible panes so viewers could read comments and browse coverage while the video kept playing, then iterated those interactions through working front-end prototypes to help define backend requirements into production.

### Support tools in the age of A.I. prototyping

I maintain [WorkBay](/projects/workbay/) keeps project decisions and review findings available across sessions with AI coding tools. The main advantage is avoiding vendor lock-in, I can seamlessly switch a session from Codex, Claude, Grok, etc. WorkBay also has a [semantic search](/research/agent-memory/) feature that surfaces previous failures and design decisions, and expands the context during planning sessions [well beyond the immediate task horizon](/research/interrogating-agent-memory/).

Another project I upkeep is [Heuristics Canon](/research/heuristics-canon/), a collection of principles drawn from published work on design and software. It's proven helpful when reviewing a proposed solution and looking for alternatives.
