## What the corpus is

The Heuristics Canon is a working research corpus. Recurring product, interaction, accessibility, AI, and engineering decisions are written as checkable rules, each with a stable ID and source provenance.

A design claim can point to a rule, a source, and the artifact that changed because of it. A critique can name the exact rule it leans on and the source behind it.

The corpus is cited by rule ID inside product cases on this site when a rule changed a decision. It decided the visible media-pending state in Semantic Image Search.

The public repository is [Heuristics Canon on GitHub](https://github.com/darce/heuristics-canon).
