## How a rule is written

Every rule names a trigger, the check that trigger forces, and a source. The trigger is the move you were about to make; the check is the question that move has to survive; the source is the study, specification, or shipped artifact that earns the rule its place. A rule with no source does not enter the corpus.

## Why the IDs are stable

The IDs do not get renumbered, so a critique written against an earlier revision still resolves. That is the difference between a style guide and a corpus you can cite: a design claim points to a rule, the rule points to a source, and the artifact records which rule changed it.

## Where it is used

The canon is cited by rule ID inside the product cases on this site, but only where a rule changed a decision. It is what put the visible media-pending state into Semantic Image Search instead of letting the case imply screenshots that do not exist.

The corpus is public: [Heuristics Canon on GitHub](https://github.com/darce/heuristics-canon).
